Windows Into Onarchy portable runtime source offer
==================================================

The portable runtime contains QEMU 11.1.0 and Zstandard 1.5.7. QEMU is
distributed under GPL-2.0 as a whole; Zstandard is dual licensed under
BSD-3-Clause or GPL-2.0. The binary payload also contains separately licensed
libraries, firmware and data supplied by the identified upstream QEMU for
Windows build.

The release must publish `windows-into-onarchy-runtime-source.zip` beside the
binary runtime. That archive contains the exact digest-pinned QEMU and
Zstandard source releases plus the source and licence manifests. The complete
unmodified upstream installer is identified by SHA-512 in provenance.json.

For three years after a binary release, corresponding source for the exact
released runtime may also be requested at:

  https://github.com/tcballard/windows-into-omarchy/issues

This written offer is a release obligation. A maintainer must not publish the
binary archive without the matching source archive, SBOM, provenance,
licence manifest, payload manifest and SHA256SUMS generated in the same run.

No endorsement by QEMU, Zstandard, their authors, or their trademark owners
is claimed.
